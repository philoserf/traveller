## What this is

A Referee’s Notebook is a set of dated posts on 1977 Classic Traveller: the Little Black Books as Game Designers’ Workshop first published them, before the Official Traveller Universe filled in the map. The posts are about running and playing those rules. Later editions appear only as commentary on what came after, never as the subject of a post’s rules or play.
